Golden hour

Stay for the sunset. Stay up for the stars.

Walk behind the main house to the back pasture, pull up an Adirondack chair, and watch the sun drop behind Sugarloaf Mountain. The skies here are usually clear, so the show keeps going after dark.

  • Panoramic views of Sugarloaf Mountain
  • Clear night skies for stargazing
  • Firewood provided by your hosts
  • Only about an hour from Washington, DC

Before you arrive

Good to know.

Drive a little further

Continue about a quarter mile past where your GPS stops. Look for the black gates and the Comus Sky Farm sign.

Rigs under 35 ft

RVs, trailers and vans up to 35 feet fit well. RV parking is by the barn, tents go on the lawn.

Dog park visitors

A private dog park runs on the land. When visitors arrive you'll be told to secure your pets, usually for about an hour.

Support the rescues

A donation box in the barn helps feed the farm's always hungry rescue animals.

Easy on site payments

Pay for extras and farm market goods with Venmo, Zelle or the QR codes in the tack room.

Private address

The exact address is shared once your booking is confirmed, to respect the host's privacy.
